Company Profile & Heritage

An Accountable Partner Built to Adapt to Your Work Culture.

Established in 2016, Praneeth Soft Solutions Pvt Ltd (PSS Global) is an enterprise consulting and services company built on the conviction that no two organizations are identical. We design software, workforce, and operational frameworks around how your business actually thrives.

Our Heritage

Delivering Results That Endure Since 2016

Over the years, PSS has been fortunate to partner with multiple corporate leaders, public sector bodies, and growing ventures across India and the United States.

With years of proven expertise in corporate consulting and talent sourcing, PSSGLOBAL was established in January 2017 with a well-defined mandate to support the US recruitment industry, procuring the right specialists for global clients across technical and functional domains.

Headquartered in Hyderabad with a strategic technology and operations branch in Bangalore, PSS combines rigorous business-process knowledge with best-practice delivery methodologies. Today, our venture into custom Software Development & Digital Engineering marks the next stage of our evolution — giving clients both the technical engineering muscle and the talent pipeline to scale it.
