Corporate Heritage

Corporate Consulting & Talent Solutions.

Established in 2016, Praneeth Soft Solutions Pvt Ltd (PSS) is a corporate consulting company offering manpower services, HR, and business solutions tailored to organizational requirements.

Our Background

Built on Practical Expertise and Enduring Relationships

Praneeth Soft Solutions Pvt Ltd was established in 2016 to deliver high-quality manpower services and effective HR and business solutions. Over the years, we have partnered with multiple organizations to fulfill their critical hiring and operational requirements.

With years of expertise in staffing, PSSGLOBAL was formed in January 2017 with a well-defined objective of serving the US recruitment industry by procuring the right people for our clients. Since then, we have provided recruitment solutions across multiple industry verticals for India and the United States.

Our specialized expertise in the evaluation and screening of manpower ensures that clients receive competent candidates who match their technical and organizational requirements. Headquartered in Hyderabad with a branch in Bangalore, PSS follows well-defined methodologies and disciplined quality procedures.
